# Project instructions

## Model-based chapters

Before editing a chapter in `content/docs/04-model-based/`, read
[`editorial/model-based/README.md`](editorial/model-based/README.md).
Compare the primary chapter with both archived variants linked in its row before
choosing an outline or rewriting its content. Read any linked chapter review notes.

`content/docs/04-model-based/` contains the publishable draft. Keep the original
snapshots in `editorial/model-based/variants/` unchanged. They are reference
material and must stay outside the site's content, search, and generated assets.

Use the detailed handbook style: explain the problem, define notation and
assumptions, derive the important equations, and connect them to worked examples
and algorithms. Record editorial choices and review progress in the guide or its
chapter notes. Keep chapter order, internal transitions, the homepage, and README
consistent when changing the reading sequence.
